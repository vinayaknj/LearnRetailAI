Module 4: Payments, Compliance, and Risk
Purpose

A retail transaction is not complete when the customer leaves the store.

The retailer must:

Successfully authorize payment
Capture funds
Settle with financial institutions
Reconcile transactions
Detect fraud
Protect sensitive customer data
Meet regulatory and compliance requirements

A store may survive inventory issues for days.

A widespread payment outage can immediately stop revenue generation.

This module provides the domain knowledge needed to understand payment systems, payment failures, compliance requirements, risk management, fraud detection, and operational response.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

✅ Explain end-to-end retail payment processing

✅ Distinguish authorization, capture, settlement, and reconciliation

✅ Understand common payment failure scenarios

✅ Explain PCI compliance boundaries

✅ Identify fraud indicators

✅ Classify payment incidents by severity

✅ Create payment response playbooks

✅ Design AI agents for payment intelligence and incident triage
