Module 6: AI Pods Applied to Retail Domain Problems
Purpose

Modules 1–5 built your retail domain expertise.

This module focuses on applying AI Pods (Multi-Agent Systems) to solve real retail operational problems safely and measurably.

The goal is not to build generic chatbots.

The goal is to build AI systems that:

Understand retail context
Use retail taxonomies
Follow operational runbooks
Respect approval boundaries
Improve KPIs
Reduce operational workload

By the end of this module, you should be able to design domain-aware AI systems that augment retail operations without introducing unnecessary risk.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

✅ Identify high-value retail AI opportunities

✅ Design domain-specific AI agents

✅ Define safe and unsafe autonomous actions

✅ Create multi-agent workflows

✅ Measure AI effectiveness using retail KPIs

✅ Build AI pods for operational support

✅ Evaluate AI recommendations against domain rules

✅ Implement approval-gated automation
