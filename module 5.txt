Module 5: Retail SRE and Support Operations
Purpose

Retail systems are only valuable when they are available, reliable, and recoverable.

A perfectly designed POS platform still fails if:

Stores cannot process transactions
Inventory updates stop flowing
Payments intermittently fail
Support teams cannot diagnose incidents quickly
Runbooks are outdated
Escalations are delayed

This module focuses on the operational side of retail technology.

You'll learn how production incidents are managed, escalated, diagnosed, resolved, and prevented in large retail environments.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

✅ Classify retail production incidents

✅ Understand support organization structures

✅ Explain MTTA, MTTR, and other operational KPIs

✅ Design triage and escalation workflows

✅ Create production-ready runbooks

✅ Perform root cause analysis (RCA)

✅ Translate incidents into business impact

✅ Design AI-powered operational support systems

Why Retail Reliability Matters

Unlike many industries, retail loses money immediately when systems fail.

Example:

Plain Text
POS Outage
↓
Customers Cannot Checkout
↓
Revenue Stops
↓
Abandoned Purchases
↓
Customer Frustration

A one-hour outage across hundreds of stores can result in substantial revenue loss.
