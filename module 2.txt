Module 2: Core Retail Transactions and Data Semantics
Purpose

Every retail operation ultimately revolves around one thing:

The transaction.

Inventory exists to support transactions. Promotions exist to influence transactions. Payments exist to complete transactions. Financial systems exist to account for transactions.

A deep understanding of transaction processing is the foundation of retail domain expertise.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

Explain the complete basket lifecycle from scan to receipt
Understand pricing, promotions, taxes, and tender processing
Distinguish returns, refunds, exchanges, voids, and cancellations
Understand SKU, UOM, hierarchy, catalog, and price-book concepts
Identify common retail failure patterns
Translate retail business rules into system behavior
Understand business impact when transaction processing fails

Example:

Plain Text

Refund

Example:

Plain Text

One item replaced with another.

Example:

Plain Text

↓

Inventory Impact:

Plain Text
Medium +1

Cancellation

Sale never completed.

Example:

Plain Text

Void

Removes item before completion.

Example:

Plain Text

Sale continues.
